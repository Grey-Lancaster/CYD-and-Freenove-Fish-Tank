About

An animated fake aquarium for the Freenove FNK0104S: a blue tang, clownfish, angelfish, goldfish and a school of neon tetras swim around a tank with rising bubbles, swaying kelp and drifting light rays. Tap the screen to drop fish food — the fish swim over and eat it (with a sonar ring and a ding ding). Looping bubble sound through the speaker, plus a clock and date in the corner. BOOT shows the IP and status.

Version v1.0.0. This flasher build runs standalone. To get the web settings page (sound, volume, rotation, auto feed, 12/24-hour time, hostname, screenshots), WiFi time sync, OTA updates and WiFi motion sleep, build from source with your WiFi details in src/secrets.h (see the README).

Source, design files and build notes: CYD-and-Freenove-Fish-Tank. A smaller CYD (320×240) version is planned.
